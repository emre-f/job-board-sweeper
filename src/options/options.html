Filtering

Timeout bucket

Put a company in timeout after you apply, so its reposts don't tempt you again (many companies limit how often you can apply - 90 days is common). Its jobs are hidden/dimmed like a blocked company until the timeout ends; then it leaves the bucket by itself. Add companies here, from the popup, or with Label company on any job card. Works on every supported site.

Debug

Categories & blocked companies

Matching is exact: an entry only blocks a company whose displayed name is identical (whitespace aside) - JobRight AI does not block "Jobright.ai". Add one entry per name variant, or use * wildcards for deliberately looser, case-insensitive matching, e.g. Jobright* or *staffing*.

Shipped categories come with the extension and apply to every user - to change those for everyone, edit src/common/constants.js or run npm run promote -- <category-id> "Company". Everything you change here (disabling categories or entries, your additions, custom categories) is only for you.
